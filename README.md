# The-New-Lord

THE NEW LORD
A vampire noir RPG in the tradition of Disco Elysium.
You wake on the floor of a butcher's walk-in freezer at 4:14 on a Monday morning. Beside you is a pile of ash that used to be the Sheriff of the Marrow Ward, three hundred years old and very hard to kill. His blood is in your veins. His coat is on your back. And his voice is in your head, along with fifteen others.
The Court says you murdered him. You have five nights to prove what really happened before they put you in the sun.
Sixteen voices, one skull. Your skills are personalities who interrupt, argue, flatter and lie to you. Parasite hears heartbeats through walls. Pedigree is an insufferable snob. Sundial can hear the dawn coming. Raise them, and they get louder.
Ten bloodlines, twenty faces. Look in the mirror and find out what you've become. Pick from ten clans, each a man or a woman with their own painted portrait, powers and curse. The Sovereign command. The Glass hear thoughts. The Hollow have no reflection at all.
A city that remembers you. Port Vellum is rain, neon and old brick. You'll meet a stand-up comedian who survived the freezer with you, a detective chasing seven missing patients, a driver with forty-one years of secrets, and a rat queen who lives in the sewer pipes. Every one of them has an opinion about you. Most of them are right.
Your choices, your consequences. Over 250,000 words of branching story. You can feed or starve, lie or confess, save people or spend them. A dozen endings decide who rules the Ward, and whether you ever see the sun again.
Painted portraits for the whole cast
A hand-drawn map of the Marrow Ward
An original 21-track noir soundtrack
Thought Cabinet–style ideas that change who you are
Eight save slots and an autosave
Works on phones in portrait or landscape, and on computers
The blood remembers. So will you.
